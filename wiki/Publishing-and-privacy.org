* Which pages are published

- *Logseq graph:* opt-in — only pages with =#+PUBLIC: true= (Org) or =public:: true= (Markdown), unless =all_public = true= (or Logseq's =:publishing/all-pages-public?=).
- *Org-mode project:* opt-out — every page, except those marked private below. =all_public = false= restores the opt-in rule.

=#+PUBLIC: false= (Org) or =public:: false= (Markdown) keep a page private when every page is published.

* Keeping notes private: noexport

The =noexport= tag keeps a note out of the site, whatever the publishing mode:

| | To hide a whole page | To hide part of a page |
|-+----------------------+------------------------|
| Emacs org-mode, org-roam, Orgamnesia | =#+FILETAGS: :noexport:= | tag the headline: =* Brouillon :noexport:= — the headline and everything under it are left out |
| Logseq, Org page | =#+filetags: :noexport:= (what Logseq writes when you add the =noexport= tag to the page) | same as Emacs: =* Brouillon :noexport:= |
| Logseq, Markdown page | =tags:: noexport= in the first block (the page properties) | — |

* Hiding folders and files

To leave out folders or files, list them in =hidden=, relative to the project root:

#+BEGIN_SRC toml
[site]
hidden = ["/archives", "/pages/drafts", "/pages/secret.org"]
#+END_SRC

A Logseq graph fills =hidden= from =:hidden= in =logseq/config.edn=. Some folders are never read anyway — see [[Project-types][Project types]].
