* Themes

Three built-in themes — =default=, =dark= and =nord= — or bring your own CSS:

#+BEGIN_SRC toml
[site]
theme = "nord"            # or "path/to/my-theme.css"
#+END_SRC

or =--theme NAME_OR_PATH= on the command line.

* Tree view

The optional *tree view* (=tree_view = true=, shown on =default= and =nord= above) draws guide lines between nested bullets and makes every branch foldable; turn it off for classic bullets (=dark= above).

* Share & print bar

Every page gets a *share & print bar* under its title, or pinned to the right edge with =layout = "vertical"=: WhatsApp, Facebook, X, Bluesky, LinkedIn, e-mail, copy link, and the native share sheet on mobile. Each button can be switched off from the =[share]= table of the TOML.

Printing a page gives a clean view without the menu, ending with the site title and address.

* Nav menu, social links and random page

The nav menu is a list of =[[menu]]= entries in the TOML; social links go in =[social_networks]= (or =--social NAME:URL=).

A menu entry with =random = true= links to a random published page, under the =label= you give it.

* Search

Full-text search runs in the browser (Fuse.js): no server needed.

* Custom pages and external sites

- *Custom HTML/CSS pages* are copied as-is (embeds, mini-apps, slide decks…).
- *External static sites* — mini-sites that live outside the project — are copied in with =[[external_static_dirs]]=.
