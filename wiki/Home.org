*The org-mode static website-builder. Bloom your personal wiki and/or blog with vanilla emacs, logseq, org-mode or Orgamnesia.*

org-bloom turns a folder of notes into a static website — HTML + CSS + vanilla JS, no server, no build framework.

| Works with      | What it reads                                                                                                          |
|-----------------+------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| *Emacs org-mode* | any folder of =.org= files, subfolders included: =#+TITLE=, =#+DATE=, =#+AUTHOR=, =#+FILETAGS=, headline tags, timestamps, =:noexport:=, =file:= links… |
| *org-roam*      | =id:= links (file and headline IDs), =daily/= notes as blog posts                                                      |
| *Logseq*        | graphs in Org *or Markdown*: wiki links, =#tags=, =tags::=, journals, =logseq/config.edn= settings                     |
| *Orgamnesia*    | its projects as is, and its Export button (=org-bloom build <project>=)                                               |
